## to understand whether the feature is numerical or categorical
Numerical data represents quantities. You can perform mathematical operations on it, such as addition, subtraction, averaging and multiplication.
examples: age, salary, height, monthly charges, tenure

Categorical data represents labels or groups, not quantities.
examples: gender, contract, internet sercvice, churn, payment method

it doesnt make sense to calculate male + female or average gender.

The main question is: can I meaningfully calculate an average?
If yes, it's usually numerical.
If no, it's usually categorical.


#### common point of confusion:
If we have the column called as CustomerID and we have the data as 1000, 1001, 1002, 1003 etc. They are categorical identifiers because they are simply labels for customers.
Another example of it is PINCODE.
